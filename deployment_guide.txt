EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: HOW AN OTT PLATFORM WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To run a streaming app like Netflix, we need more than just the mobile app. 
Think of it like setting up a physical cinema hall:
1. The Mobile App is the "Cinema Hall Door".
2. The Server & Database is the "Ticket Counter" (remembers user profiles, logins, 
   and plans).
3. Cloud Storage is the "Film Reel Warehouse" (where video files are safely kept).
4. CDN is the "Local Projector Rooms" (makes sure videos play instantly near the 
   user without loading/buffering, even on a slow mobile connection).
5. DRM is the "Security Guard" (stops users from screen-recording or stealing 
   our movies).

PART 1: GOVERNMENT APPROVALS, LICENSES & SECURITY (THE "PERMISSIONS" COST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To list the app on App Stores and show movies legally, we must pay for these 
setup fees and licensing:

1. Google Play Console Account
   - What it is: Permission to list our app on the Android Play Store.
   - Cost: Rs. 2,100 (One-time payment to Google).

2. Apple Developer Account
   - What it is: Permission to list our app on the iOS App Store.
   - Cost: Rs. 8,700 per year (Annual subscription to Apple).

3. Government Registration (IT Rules, 2021)
   - What it is: Under Indian law, all video platforms must register with the 
     Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) and join a Grievance Council 
     (like DPCGC) to handle complaints about content age-ratings (U, 13+, 18+).
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 50,000 per year (Membership fee for the council).

4. DRM - Anti-Piracy Lock (Widevine & FairPlay DRM)
   - What it is: Digital security locks. Without this, users will screen-record 
     our movies and share them on Telegram. Movie distributors will NOT give us 
     their movies if we don't have DRM.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 2,500 per month (Paid to a provider like EZDRM, based on 
     number of video plays).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: THE TECH INFRASTRUCTURE (THE "DIGITAL MACHINERY" COST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are the computers (servers) in the cloud that store the data and send 
videos to users:

1. Main App Server (Node.js API host)
   - What it is: The brain of the app. It handles logins, home page carousels, 
     and searches.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 1,000 per month (We can host it on DigitalOcean or AWS).

2. Database (PostgreSQL / Supabase)
   - What it is: The digital ledger. It remembers every user's email, phone number, 
     password, and active subscription plan.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 2,100 per month (Supabase Pro plan).

3. Redis Memory Cache
   - What it is: A helper database that sits next to the main database to make 
     the app load in under 1 second.
   - Cost: Rs. 0 initially (Free tier on Upstash covers small user bases).

4. Cloud Video Storage (Cloudflare R2)
   - What it is: The warehouse where our video files are stored.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 2,500 per month (depending on how many hours of movies 
     we upload. Cloudflare R2 is chosen because it has ZERO download charges, 
     whereas AWS S3 will charge heavy fees every time a user watches a video).

5. CDN - Buffering Prevention Network (Cloudflare CDN)
   - What it is: Copier servers placed all over India. If a user in Mumbai plays 
     a movie, the video is sent from a Mumbai server, preventing buffer circles.
   - Cost: Rs. 0 (Cloudflare's basic CDN tier is free).

PART 3: VIDEO PROCESSING & SMART FEATURES (THE "NETFLIX EXPERIENCE" COST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To give the app a premium feel with OTP login and automatic recommendation lists:

1. Video Transcoding (Auto-resolution adjustment)
   - What it is: When we upload a raw 4K movie, this software automatically 
     converts it into 1080p, 720p, and 480p versions so it plays smoothly 
     even on slow Jio mobile networks.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 3,000 per month (AWS Elemental MediaConvert, based on 
     minutes of video processed).

2. SMS OTP Gateway (Twilio / MSG91)
   - What it is: Sending verification codes to user mobile phones during signup 
     to prevent fake accounts.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 0.20 per SMS (Pay-as-you-go. For 5,000 signups/month, 
     cost is Rs. 1,000/month).

3. Email Service (Amazon SES)
   - What it is: Sending welcome emails, subscription bills, and support emails.
   - Cost: Rs. 0 (Free up to 3,000 emails/month, then Rs. 8.30 per 1,000 emails).

4. TV Testing Devices
   - What it is: Physical Smart TV sticks (FireStick, Apple TV box, Chromecast) 
     needed to test the app before launching on TVs.
   - Cost: approx. Rs. 20,000 (One-time purchase of testing hardware).

PART 4: INDIAN MOBILE BILLING & GST TAXES (RULES & COMMISSIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Operating a subscription platform in India requires following RBI bank rules and 
tax laws:

1. Apple & Google Store Cuts (App Store Purchases)
   - Rule: For subscriptions bought inside the mobile app, Google and Apple 
     take a 15% cut of our sale price.
   - User Choice Billing: Under new Indian CCI rules, we can offer local UPI 
     inside the app. If the user pays using our local gateway (like Razorpay), 
     Google's cut is reduced to 11%, saving us money.

2. RBI Recurring Payments Rules (Auto-Pay)
   - Rule: To charge a user automatically every month (like Netflix does), we 
     MUST use an RBI-approved payment gateway (Razorpay Subscriptions).
   - Process: Razorpay will automatically send an SMS and email to the user 
     24 hours before debiting money. Any debit above Rs. 15,000 will require 
     OTP approval.

3. GST Tax on Digital Subscriptions (OIDAR)
   - Rule: The government charges 18% GST on digital streaming services.
   - Display Rule: The price shown to users must include GST (e.g., if we charge 
     Rs. 299, the tax of Rs. 45.61 is already included).
   - Tax Filing: We must file GST returns every month (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B).
